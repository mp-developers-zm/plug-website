  1. Add products to your cart. You can review it, change quantities or remove items at any time before you place your order.

Payment options are shown at checkout (Mobile Money, card, bank transfer, or paying in store when you collect). Card and Mobile Money payments go through a secure payment provider. Plug will never ask for your PIN or full card details on WhatsApp.

Delivery and collection

  • Delivery is available in Lusaka and Ndola.
