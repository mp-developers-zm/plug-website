Featured vehicle2022 Ford Ranger

The Ranger name has been on Ford pickups since 1983. This generation, launched in 2022, was led by Ford’s product development centre in Australia, with about 2,000 designers and engineers, and is built in Thailand and South Africa. Engines run from 2.0-litre single- and bi-turbo diesels to a 3.0-litre V6 turbo-diesel with 184 kW and 600 Nm, paired with a 10-speed automatic. On the rally stage, Ford raced the Ranger T1+ at the 2024 Dakar Rally, and the Ranger-inspired Raptor T1+ took third on its 2025 debut, then second and third overall in 2026.
